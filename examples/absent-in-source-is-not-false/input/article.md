# Maple Leaf Zoo with Kids: Prices, Hours and Tips

Maple Leaf Zoo is the best zoo in the region for families with toddlers.

## Tickets and hours

Adult admission is $18, and children aged 3 to 12 pay $12.
Children under 3 get in free.
The zoo opens at 9:00 a.m. and closes at 5:00 p.m. every day.

## Facilities

The zoo has a splash pad that opens in summer.
There is no nursing room at Maple Leaf Zoo, so plan to feed your baby before you arrive.
Stroller rental is available at the main gate.

## Our visit

When we visited last spring, my kids loved feeding the giraffes at the Savanna Deck.
According to a survey, 9 out of 10 parents say the zoo is stroller-friendly.

## Before you go

Annual passes are on sale until December 31, 2026.
