== Prices ==
Admission: Adults $22
Children (3-12): $12
Under 3: free
Annual pass: Adults $95, Children $55

== Hours ==
Open daily 9:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. Last entry 4:00 p.m.
Closed on December 25.

== Facilities (as listed on the Facilities page) ==
Restrooms at the main gate and the Savanna Deck
Stroller rental at the main gate ($8 per day)
Gift shop and Treetop Cafe
Picnic area near the Lake Trail
Lockers at the main gate
